# Interview Prep

Read `../../references/common.md`. Accept the user's current documents directly.
Research and cover-letter generation are optional, not prerequisites.

## Workflow

1. Establish role, interview stage, likely audience, language, date if relevant,
   and whether the user wants a prep pack or a mock interview. With no exact
   role, prepare general role-family questions and label assumptions.
2. Map job criteria to direct evidence, transferable evidence, and areas to
   clarify. Missing details are unconfirmed, not proof of a weakness.
3. Build 3-5 STAR/CAR story outlines using the candidate's actual contribution.
   Keep unknown metrics out of polished answers; use concrete qualitative
   outcomes. Distinguish completed work from hypothetical approaches.
4. Provide likely technical/behavioral questions, follow-ups, truthful gap or
   career-change explanations, and informed questions for the interviewer.
   Use optional company research to sharpen context, not to claim knowledge of
   private interview questions or internal hiring decisions.
5. Include compensation/availability preparation only when relevant or requested.
   Keep the user's actual constraints and units. Do not invent market ranges.
6. In mock mode, ask one question at a time, wait for the answer, then give brief
   feedback on relevance, evidence, clarity, and ownership before continuing.

Deliver a focused prep pack, not a memorization script. Use English by default
and preserve the candidate's voice. Offer research only when the user wants
additional employer context; never browse or install dependencies automatically.
