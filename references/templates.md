# ATS-Safe Document Templates

These are content and reading-order templates, not visual design instructions.
For Germany, use A4 and implement date/content alignment with tab stops or
indentation rather than table objects or sidebars.

## German Tabellarischer Lebenslauf

```text
VORNAME NACHNAME
City, Country | +country phone | email | LinkedIn/portfolio

KURZPROFIL
Target role, experience level, two or three substantiated strengths, and target
direction. Keep to two to four lines.

BERUFSERFAHRUNG
MM/YYYY - MM/YYYY    Job title | Employer | City, Country
                     - Action + tool/context + scope + measurable result.
                     - Action + responsibility + outcome.

MM/YYYY - MM/YYYY    Previous role | Employer | City, Country
                     - Relevant evidence only.

AUSBILDUNG
MM/YYYY - MM/YYYY    Degree / vocational qualification | Institution | City
                     Relevant focus, thesis, or grade only when useful.

KENNTNISSE
Tools and systems: ...
Methods and domain: ...
Sprachen: [language] ([CEFR level]), ...   # candidate's real, honest levels

ZERTIFIKATE / PROJEKTE / EHRENAMT
Only relevant evidence.

Ort, Datum
Unterschrift, Name              # use when appropriate for the process
```

Do not implement the displayed alignment with a Word table. For a strict ATS
variant, put each date on its own line above the role and keep all content in one
left-aligned flow.

## Student / Werkstudent / Praktikum CV

```text
NAME
City | Phone | Email | LinkedIn/GitHub

KURZPROFIL
Degree, target role, strongest relevant project or practical experience.

AUSBILDUNG / STUDIUM
MM/YYYY - present    Degree, subject | University
                     Expected graduation: MM/YYYY | Relevant focus: ...

PROJEKTE / PRAKTISCHE ERFAHRUNG
MM/YYYY - MM/YYYY    Project / Praktikum / Werkstudent role
                     - Task, personal contribution, tool, result.

KENNTNISSE
Programming / tools: ...
Methods: ...
Languages: ...

VERFÜGBARKEIT
Available from ... | ... hours/week, only if requested or useful.

ZERTIFIKATE / ENGAGEMENT
Only relevant entries.
```

Use a one-page target. A second page is justified only by meaningful additional
evidence, not by generic hobbies or every school detail.

## English Tech CV for a German Employer

```text
NAME
City, Country | Phone | Email | LinkedIn | GitHub/portfolio

SUMMARY
Target role, years or level, strongest stack, and one evidence-led value claim.

TECHNICAL SKILLS
Languages: ...
Frameworks/platforms: ...
Cloud/data/DevOps: ...
Methods/certifications: ...

EXPERIENCE
MM/YYYY - present    Role | Employer | Location
                     - Built/led/optimized [what] with [tool/scope], resulting in [outcome].

EDUCATION
Degree | Institution | Year

LANGUAGES
German (CEFR), English (CEFR), ...

PROJECTS / OPEN SOURCE
Only work that proves the target requirements.
```

Use the ad's terminology and omit photo/DOB by default. Keep the document
single-column even when the employer uses Greenhouse, Lever, or Ashby.

## German Anschreiben

```text
NAME | Address or city | Phone | Email
Company
Contact person
Company address

City, Date

Bewerbung als [exact job title], Referenz [if supplied]

Sehr geehrte Frau [Name] / Sehr geehrter Herr [Name],

Direct reason for applying and one-line positioning for the role.

One concrete achievement that proves the most important requirement. Name the
tool, scale, responsibility, and result; do not repeat the full CV.

Why this employer/role, plus earliest realistic start date and salary
expectation only if requested.

Mit freundlichen Grüßen

Signature
NAME
```

If no contact is reliably known, use a neutral formal salutation rather than
guessing a person's name. Use a short portal motivation answer instead when the
employer explicitly replaces the letter with questions.

## Short Application Email

```text
Subject: Application for [exact role] - [name]

Dear [name] / Hello,

I am applying for [role]. My relevant evidence is [one concrete result].
I am available [date] and have attached [documents].

Kind regards,
Name | Phone | Email
```

## Attachment Plan

```text
1. Anschreiben (only when required/useful)
2. Lebenslauf / CV
3. Latest relevant Arbeitszeugnis or work reference
4. Highest relevant degree/vocational certificate
5. Relevant certificates, portfolio, or work samples
```

Use separate portal fields when available. If a single PDF is requested, merge
only relevant documents, check readability, and use a clear filename such as
`FirstName_LastName_Application_Role.pdf`.
