# Germany Application Standards

Use this reference when the target role is in Germany or the job materials use
German application terms. The guidance is written in English. Generated
documents default to English; use German when the user requests it or the
employer requires German-language documents. Preserve German advertisement
terminology in the keyword map either way.

Most rules here are Germany-specific. Austria is broadly similar; Switzerland
differs (region-dependent language, local spelling). Do not generalise a German
legal, salary, or document rule to Austria or Switzerland without checking.

## Market Decision Matrix

| Signal | Default decision |
| --- | --- |
| German-language advertisement | Keep English documents by default (project policy) and note the language expectation; switch to German when required or requested. Use German keywords either way |
| English-language advertisement at an international employer | English CV/cover letter; list German level honestly |
| Public sector, authority, clinic, or formal tariff employer | Complete, formal package; exact requirement mapping; Anschreiben usually expected |
| Classic Mittelstand, family business, client-facing role | Tabellarischer Lebenslauf structure; photo is optional but may be culturally expected |
| Large enterprise / regulated employer portal | Strict ATS-safe version; no decorative layout. If the specific ATS is unknown, apply the general parse-safe defaults rather than assuming a named system |
| Berlin tech or international startup | English often acceptable; skills and outcomes forward; omit photo/DOB unless requested |
| Student, Werkstudent, Praktikum, or graduate | One page; education or strongest relevant project first; practical evidence immediately visible |
| Direct referral or human-first email | A designed version can be retained, but keep an ATS-safe version for portals |

## The German CV Without the ATS Trap

`Tabellarischer Lebenslauf` means a structured CV with dates visually separated
from content. It does **not** mean an actual Word table, HTML table, sidebar, or
floating text boxes.

Build the document as one linear text flow:

```text
06/2022 - 08/2025    Software Engineer | Example GmbH | Berlin
                      - Built ...
                      - Reduced ...
```

Use tab stops or stable indentation in a document editor. Then run the
copy/paste test against a plain-text editor. If the dates, role, employer, or
bullets appear out of order, rebuild it as a stricter single-column document.

## Standard Content Choices

### Usually include

- Name, reachable email, phone, and location relevant to the role.
- Professional profile when it adds clear positioning.
- Reverse-chronological work experience and education.
- Relevant tools, methods, languages with CEFR levels, certifications, and
  projects.
- Honest labels for gaps and transitions.

### Context-dependent

- Full postal address, date of birth, nationality, photo, signature, hobbies,
  grade, and work-permit status.
- Include only when the process, sector, role, or candidate preference makes the
  information useful. Do not treat traditional practice as a legal requirement.

### Normally omit

- Marital status, children, religion, parents, health information, political
  details, unrelated personal data, and "references available upon request".

## Career-Stage Variants

### Students and early-career applicants

- One page is the normal target.
- Put `Ausbildung / Studium` first when it is the strongest qualification.
- Add relevant modules, expected graduation, a good grade, thesis, projects,
  Praktika, HiWi/Tutorium, student initiatives, and relevant work.
- Turn every project into evidence: task, personal contribution, tool, result.
- Add availability or weekly hours only when the job asks for them.
- Include an Immatrikulationsbescheinigung or transcript as an attachment only
  when requested or useful; do not crowd the CV with document detail.

### Experienced professionals

- Put Berufserfahrung first and focus detail on recent, relevant roles.
- One to two pages; do not compress substantive evidence to satisfy an arbitrary
  one-page rule.
- Use selected Arbeitszeugnisse and certificates as Anlagen, not a reference-list
  substitute.

### Tech and English-language applications

- Use a single-column, text-first CV with a grouped technical skills section near
  the top and impact-led experience bullets.
- English is the default per project policy; if the advertisement/process
  requires German, flag that and offer a German version. Do not submit a literal
  word-for-word translation.
- Omit photo, DOB, marital status, and similar data by default for international
  and tech processes.
- Keep German, English, and other languages with CEFR levels; never inflate them.

## Anschreiben Rules

There is no general legal requirement for an Anschreiben. Generate one when:

- The ad asks for `Anschreiben`, `Bewerbungsschreiben`, or `vollständige
  Bewerbungsunterlagen`.
- The portal has a mandatory cover-letter or motivation field.
- The candidate needs to explain a career change, gap, relocation, leadership
  motivation, or an unsolicited application.

When optional, prefer 150-250 words with one concrete achievement over a generic
full-page letter. Use a specific contact, clear subject, direct opening,
evidence tied to the advertisement, motivation, and a closing with availability
and salary only when requested. Use DIN 5008 as a formal layout reference, not
as a legal claim.

## Attachments and Proof

Typical German Anlagen, ordered by relevance:

1. Latest or strongest qualified Arbeitszeugnis.
2. Other relevant Arbeitszeugnisse, newest first, usually limited to two or
   three.
3. Highest relevant university or vocational certificate.
4. Relevant training, language, technical, or professional certificates.
5. Portfolio, work sample, or other proof only when requested or role-relevant.

Follow the portal's separate upload fields when provided. Do not send every old
document or expose unnecessary personal data in a scan.

## International Applicants

- Keep original degree and employer names, then add a concise plain-language
  explanation when needed.
- Use `anabin` or a ZAB `Zeugnisbewertung` only when the candidate has checked
  the result; never invent German equivalence.
- Formal recognition is generally required for regulated professions, not every
  profession.
- State German and other language levels using CEFR; mention work authorization
  only when requested or when it resolves a real screening question.
- Recognition and work-authorisation rules are Germany-specific; confirm the
  destination country before applying them to Austria or Switzerland.
